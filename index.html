Delta CRM

Delta CRM is a customer relationship management application for small teams: contacts, companies, deals and projects, tasks, calls and email in one place.

The application runs at deltacrm.io. It is operated by István Bálint as a private individual.

A user can connect their own Google account so that Delta CRM can send email on their behalf, show their incoming email next to the matching customer, and keep their Google Calendar in step with their CRM appointments. What is accessed, and why, is described in the privacy policy.
